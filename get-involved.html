Ways to support the mission ↗︎

In development. Beyond Walls New Paths is a proposed nonprofit mission for women experiencing homelessness throughout the San Francisco Bay Area. Mobile showers and direct services have not launched. Online donations are not open. The proposed legal name is subject to California availability and Keisha Garrett’s final approval; incorporation and tax-exempt status are not represented as confirmed.
