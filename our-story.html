MEET THE HEART BEHIND IT

Keisha Garrett’s vision

Beyond These Walls begins with a simple intention: help people see a way forward, and help them take the next step.

A San Francisco native and Resource Navigator, Keisha Garrett brings lived experience and a commitment to hands on guidance to this mission. She chose Beyond These Walls because she believes people need more than reassurance that a new beginning is possible. They need practical help finding their way toward it.

The initial focus is women experiencing homelessness, with resource information available to everyone. Food, new clothing, showers, employment, housing information, and compassionate guidance form the starting vision. As the mission grows, Keisha hopes to expand into housing assistance and, eventually, a physical location.

That future is something we are working toward. This is the beginning of the journey.

WHAT GUIDES US

Faith in action.
Respect in every interaction.

Dignity

Every person deserves to be heard and treated with care, without being reduced to their circumstances.

Compassion

We want support to feel human, welcoming, and grounded in understanding.

Possibility

We believe there is more ahead than the barriers a person is facing today.

Keisha’s faith is central to her vision: keep belief and purpose at the heart of the journey. Beyond These Walls welcomes people from different faiths and backgrounds.
