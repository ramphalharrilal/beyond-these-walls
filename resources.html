Before you go

These are independent services, not affiliated partners of Beyond Walls New Paths. Contact each provider directly for current availability, eligibility, hours, and next steps. Links and descriptions reviewed October 17, 2026. If a directory will not load, call 211. This is not a real-time availability check. No individual opening hours, available beds, or appointments are guaranteed. For counties not listed by 211 Bay Area, use 211 California’s county directory.

Community referrals: 211 · City information: 311 · Immediate emergencies: 911

In development. Beyond Walls New Paths is a proposed nonprofit mission for women experiencing homelessness throughout the San Francisco Bay Area. Mobile showers and direct services have not launched. Online donations are not open.
